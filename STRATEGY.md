# Product Strategy: Sai Krishna Sunkari — Portfolio

**Date:** 2026-06-23
**Stage:** Live site, repositioning the homepage for conversion
**Treat as:** a product whose North Star is *a qualified visitor books a call / reaches out*

---

### 1. Vision
The clearest signal in crypto/AI that *this person has already shipped the thing you're about to build* — so a founder's decision to reach out feels less like a risk and more like relief.

### 2. Target Segments
| Segment | Pain level | Current alternative | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crypto/web3 + AI founders/teams** needing an architect / fractional eng lead | High — high-stakes, expensive to get wrong | Agencies, in-house senior hire, referrals | **Primary** |
| Employers / recruiters (senior/staff blockchain roles) | Medium | LinkedIn, job boards | Secondary |
| Peers / community (other builders) | Low | X, Medium | Tertiary |

**Primary:** the founder buyer. Risk-averse, time-poor, *technical* — trusts artifacts, not adjectives.
**Explicitly not optimizing for:** a general/non-technical audience, or "personal brand" follower growth at the expense of the booking action.

### 3. Pain Points & Value
The founder's real fear: *"Can I trust this person with my protocol's architecture and my team, or will this cost me months and money?"* The site's job is to extinguish that fear fast with **evidence**: named, shipped, production systems + an unbroken senior trajectory + recognition. Value created = compressed time-to-trust.

### 4. Value Proposition (JTBD)
**For the founder buyer:** *When* I'm deciding who to trust with high-stakes blockchain architecture and team leadership, *I want* to see — in the first scroll — that this person has personally shipped systems like mine across the chains I care about, *so I can* book a call with confidence instead of gambling on a hire.

### 5. Strategic Trade-offs
| We choose | Over | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof first** (what he built/led) | Pitch first ("what I do") | A technical buyer trusts artifacts; proof earns the right to pitch |
| **Depth** — named protocols, chains, awards, roles | Breadth of adjectives ("expert, passionate") | Specifics are credible; adjectives are noise |
| **The founder buyer's reading order** | The recruiter's reading order | Lead audience is clients; optimize for them |
| **Two heavy proof pillars** (Work + Trajectory) | Many equal-weight sections | Equal weight = no focal point = trust never lands |
| **Persistent, single CTA** (Book a call) | Multiple competing actions | One obvious next step converts |

### 6. Key Metrics
- **North Star:** qualified visitor books a call / reaches out.
- **Input metrics:** time-to-first-proof (how fast real work appears); scroll-reach into Selected Work; CTA clicks (rail + close); Writing click-through (depth signal).
- **Health/guardrails:** bounce before any proof is seen; mobile CTA reachability; readability (AA contrast, comfortable measure).

### 7. Growth Engine
Inbound by reputation: referrals, X, conference/ecosystem visibility → land on site → **proof converts** → Calendly. The site is the conversion step, not the acquisition step — so it must *close*, not *introduce*.

### 8. Core Capabilities (of the page)
Surface the real assets he already has: 13 shipped systems (2 award-winning), a 7-year intern→architect→founder arc, 10+ chains, current AI-agent work, and writing. The capability gap is **presentation/hierarchy**, not content.

### 9. Defensibility
The moat is **specificity**: the exact named protocols, the Cosmos ecosystem awards, the founder roles, the breadth of chains — none of which a generic competitor can claim. Lead with what only he can say.

## ► The deliverable: Homepage Section Priority

Grounded in the founder buyer's JTBD and "what builds trust fastest." This **validates the instinct that Proof + Trajectory + Selected Work lead** — and refines it:

| Rank | Section | Role | Visual weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Orient** — name + 1-line value prop + scannable credibility strip (7 yrs · 10+ chains · 2 awards · ecosystems) | 3-second "who + legit" | Compact, top |
| **1** | **Selected Work** (named shipped systems, outcomes, awards) | *What he built* — strongest proof | **Heaviest** |
| **2** | **Trajectory** (7-yr arc, named senior roles, founder) | *Proof of seniority & leadership* — co-pillar | **Heavy** |
| 3 | **What I do** (Architecture / Strategy / Leadership) | The offer — proof earned the pitch | Medium, concise |
| 4 | **Writing** (essays) | Thinking/communication — supporting | Light, but visible |
| 5 | **Ventures founded** | Entrepreneurial range — supporting | Light / foldable |
| — | **Contact / Book a call** | The action | **Persistent** (rail) + strong close |

**The key correction vs. earlier drafts:** Trajectory is NOT a light footnote at the bottom. For an *architect / fractional-lead* engagement, the career arc (intern → architect → founder, named companies, 7 years) is itself first-class proof of seniority. **Selected Work and Trajectory are the two heavy proof pillars, side by side at the top of the body**, after a 3-second orientation, before any "what I do" pitch. Everything else is lighter and supports them.

**One-line strategy:** *Orient in 3 seconds, then let two pillars of proof — what he built and the seniority of his arc — carry the page, with the booking action always one click away.*
