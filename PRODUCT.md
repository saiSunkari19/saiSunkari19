# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences, prioritized in this order:

1. **Prospective clients** (lead) — founders and teams building in crypto/web3 and AI who are evaluating Sai for paid work. They arrive skeptical and time-poor, scanning for proof of judgment and shipping track record before deciding to reach out. The site's primary conversion is the Calendly "Book a call" CTA.
2. **Employers / recruiters** — assessing seniority, depth, and range across a 13-project body of work.
3. **Peers & community** — other builders and founders who find Sai through his writing and X presence; here for ideas and taste, and a path to follow his work.

The job to be done, across all three: *quickly decide whether Sai is the real thing.* Every screen should shorten the distance from "who is this" to "I trust this person — let's talk."

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and writing site for Sai Krishna Sunkari that converts a cold visitor into a booked call (or a follower) by demonstrating builder credibility — not claiming it. It is a single-page editorial home (Hero, Stats, Approach, Trajectory/ventures, filterable Work grid of 13, About, Contact) plus a self-hosted `/writing` section that aggregates self-authored posts and Medium/X articles. Success looks like: a visitor who didn't know Sai leaves either with a call booked or with enough conviction to follow and come back.

## Brand Personality

**Calm, precise, technical.** Quiet authority over hype. The voice is an engineer's: confident because the work backs it up, never loud about it. Restraint and precision are the personality — clean type, deliberate spacing, gold used sparingly as punctuation, not decoration. The emotional goal is *trust through evidence*: the visitor should feel they're looking at someone who ships and sweats the details, not someone selling.

## Anti-references

This must NOT look like any of:

- **Generic dev-portfolio template** — Tailwind-default starter look, repeated icon-heading-text card grids, gradient buttons, "I'm a full-stack developer" hero. The single biggest trap to avoid.
- **Crypto/web3 hype aesthetic** — neon gradients, glassmorphism, 3D glows, token-landing-page energy. Sai works in this space; the site must read as *credible builder*, not *shill*.
- **Corporate SaaS** — sterile blue/navy, stock illustration, hero-metric template, B2B product-site sterility.
- **Loud agency portfolio** — maximalist scroll-jacking, oversized type shouting, motion for its own sake.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Credibility is demonstrated through real shipped work, writing, and specifics — never asserted with adjectives. If a claim can be replaced by evidence, replace it.
2. **Precision is the proof.** Pixel-level craft, correct details, and rigor *are* the argument for hiring a technical person. Sloppiness anywhere undermines the whole pitch.
3. **Calm confidence.** Quiet, deliberate, unhurried. The work carries the weight; the design gets out of its way. No hype, no shouting, no urgency theater.
4. **One clear next step.** Every section earns its keep by moving the visitor toward the single primary action — book a call — without crowding it or competing CTAs.
5. **Writing as evidence.** The `/writing` section is not a blog afterthought; it's proof of thinking. Treat it as a first-class credibility surface, not a content dump.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** is the floor; body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 against the dark `#0e0f12` background. Muted ramp tokens were tuned up to pass AA.
- Target **Lighthouse accessibility 100**.
- **Reduced motion is honored** — count-ups, reveals, and timeline fills have a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative; nothing essential is gated behind animation.
- Fully **responsive** (mobile, tablet, desktop) with keyboard-navigable nav, including the mobile drawer.
